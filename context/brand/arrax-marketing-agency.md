**Services:** performance marketing (Meta + Google Ads), SEO/GEO/AEO, content creation (owns physical studios in Pakistan for video/photo), TikTok marketing, paid search, social media, Google Shopping, email, influencer, CRO, Amazon marketing, plus web development and AI/automation/CRM building.

**Positioning strategy (decided):** present as a *Pakistan-based* agency serving international markets — this reads as more trustworthy than pretending to be local.
