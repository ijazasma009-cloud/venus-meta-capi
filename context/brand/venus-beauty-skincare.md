**Venus Beauty** (venusbeauty.pk) is a direct-to-consumer skincare brand in the Venus family, deliberately a **separate world** from [[venus-aesthetics-clinic]]. Sold on Shopify, PKR pricing, cash on delivery.

**The core idea, in Masroor's framing:** *"The clinic is calm. The brand is loud."* Clinic = calm, gold, quiet luxury. Brand = bright, bold, playful, colour-first. Same trust, completely different energy. That gap is the entire point.

**The correction that reset the brand:** premium is **not** luxury. He killed the Didone serif and the Venus gold from v1 and sent them back to the clinic. "Luxury dates fast." Tone is locked as *"Premium, not precious. Fun, never childish."* Thesis line: **"Skin science, in full colour."**

**Colour is the personality, not an accent.** Every product owns one bright colour that runs its card, page, banners and offers on a clean white canvas. Eight colours: Vitamin C amber, Niacinamide teal, Retinol pink, Hyaluronic blue, Glutathione violet, Sunblock cyan, Glycolic coral, Moisturizer green. He said the v2 board was "a little bit high" so the built palette is **desaturated a touch**.

**Real product names only** — Vitamin C Serum (Rs 3,000), Niacinamide Serum (Rs 3,000), Retinol Serum (Rs 3,250), Hyaluronic Acid Serum (Rs 3,250), Glutathione Serum (Rs 3,250), SPF 60 Sunblock (Rs 3,500), Moisturizer (Rs 2,850), Glycolic Acid Facewash (Rs 2,850). **Never** invented marketing names like "Glow Facial Serum". Banned words: miracle, cure, magic, luxury, timeless.

**Bundles:** Skin Brightening Rs 12,960 · Hydration Rs 9,960 · Tri Serum Rs 7,600 · Anti Hair Loss Rs 6,600.

**Two approved structures, do not redesign:** the **8-image-per-product recipe** (hero shot, in-use on skin, hero ingredient, texture macro, how-it-works infographic, before/after, what's inside, pairs-with) and the **MyVanillaSkin dedicated-product-section model** where each product runs as its own colour-themed world.

**References and what was taken from each:** Dot & Key (colour north star, dialled down), MyVanillaSkin (his own earlier build, the per-product sections), neuederma (Pakistani, the trust/content backbone), Typology (hover-still-becomes-video). Inspiration, never copies.

**Build sequence agreed:** high-fidelity HTML homepage first → approve → product page → port into a **private** Shopify preview theme. Publish nothing without his approval. Hover films come last, from the professional shoot.
