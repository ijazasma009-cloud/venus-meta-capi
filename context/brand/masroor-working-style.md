Hard rules learned across many rounds of feedback with Masroor:

1. **Never use em-dashes or en-dashes (— –) in deliverable copy.** He says it "looks like a very AI type of thing." Applies to website copy, captions, titles, meta descriptions. One rebuild required stripping 422 of them.
2. **AI-generated people are ALLOWED, but they must not read as AI.** Updated 3 Aug 2026, superseding the earlier blanket ban: "there is no issue with AI generated images or people. The only thing we will not use is images that don't look like real people." So the test is photorealism, not provenance. Reject plastic skin, dead eyes, warped hands, six-finger artefacts, and any set where the product changes shape between frames. Real photography and real client footage still win when available. What he actually rejected before was AI-*looking* output, not AI itself.
3. **Never fabricate data and pass it off as real.** He caught simulated Landed/Revenue/ROAS figures in the CRM. If data must be placeholder, label it visibly (the "SIM" badge approach worked) and say so plainly.
4. **Ask questions before building.** He explicitly asks for the multiple-choice question popups (AskUserQuestion) so he can lock decisions first. He responds well to "here are 3 dials I can turn."
5. **He rates work out of 10 and iterates hard.** Early drafts get 0/10 or 3/10. This is normal, not failure. He says "don't stop working until you think it is 100 out of 10" and wants self-looping.
6. **Conversion first, then SEO/GEO/AEO, then beauty.** Every website must be built to convert (form high on the page, WhatsApp CTA, sticky bars) AND rank on Google *and* AI engines (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini).
7. **Voice notes garble proper nouns.** Verify names against source: "Eric's Marketing" = Arrax, "Dr. Elmina" = Dr. Alvina Jalil, "I to try a low diagnostic" = Al Noor Diagnostic, "Pobao" = Pabau, "Yost SEO" = Yoast SEO, "Nova Mirra" = Novamira.
8. **Design references he loves:** LaserAway (No.1 reference, especially its step-by-step form), Peachy Studio, Lucia Clinic, The Nova Clinic, Dot & Key (colour), Apple/Rolls-Royce (scroll animation), thebasementbuilders.ca. He wants inspiration, explicitly not copies.
9. **He wants heavy scroll animation and full-width layouts.** GSAP + ScrollTrigger + Lenis is the stack that satisfied him. Static pages get rejected as "very static."
10. **Be honest about timelines.** He asked for national #1 rankings in a month; stating plainly that local + AI wins land in 30-45 days while national takes 90-180 days was well received and positions us as trustworthy.
11. **Answer in chat; do not build a document for every reply.** Told directly on 13 Aug 2026: "please dont create doc in every time when the con is small give me ans in chat." Reserve artifacts/reports for genuine deliverables he will act on or forward to a client. Short answers, status checks, and findings that fit in a few paragraphs belong in the chat reply.

**Why:** He is a marketing agency owner reselling this work to his own clients, so anything that reads as AI-generated, fabricated, or generic damages his credibility with paying customers.

**How to apply:** Before delivering, sweep for dashes, check every human image is real, label any estimated number, and lead the response with what changed against his last feedback list, point by point.
