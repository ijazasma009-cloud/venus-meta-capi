**Venus Aesthetics** (venusaesthetics.pk) is a premium aesthetic clinic chain in Pakistan and an Arrax Marketing client ([[arrax-marketing-agency]]). Founder & CEO: **Dr. Uzair**. Roughly 8 years operating, 8 branches.

**Branches (confirmed):** Lahore (MM Alam, Faisal Town, DHA, Lake City), Karachi (Shaheed-e-Millat), Islamabad (F7), Faisalabad, Gujranwala. **No Multan branch** — this was a repeated correction. Lake City is treated internally as the luxury/flagship branch and is used for private shoots and influencer days; do not label it "luxury" publicly.

**Treatment categories and the devices behind them** (learned directly from Dr. Uzair's briefs):
- **Skin Rejuvenation** — Venus Viva RF microneedling. Sub-treatments: Venus Scarfy, acne scar, surgical/traumatic scar, Venus Clear, Venus Anti-Wrinkle, stretch mark removal, rosacea.
- **Skin Tightening** — Venus Legacy. Sub-treatments: neck, face, butt tightening, bingo wings.
- **Laser Hair Removal** — Primelase HR Excellence with Crystal Freeze cooling.
- **Facials** — Venus Glow (HydraFacial), Party Peel, AcneOUT Peel, Exosome Peel.
- **Fat Reduction & Weight Loss** — Venus Fat Freeze (cryolipolysis, "lose up to 5 inches", no surgery/needles/downtime) and **Venus SlimFit** (doctor-supervised Tirzepatide weight loss, from ~Rs 9,500/session).
- **Botox** — its own category, not lumped under "injectables". Sub-uses: wrinkles, fine lines, masseter, gummy smile, lip flip, hyperhidrosis/sweating.
- **Hair** — PRP, Fusion PRP, Exosome hair therapy, Scalp Detox.
- **Dermatology** — must be ONE page covering acne, melasma, eczema, psoriasis, warts/moles/skin tags, alopecia. Not separate pages.
