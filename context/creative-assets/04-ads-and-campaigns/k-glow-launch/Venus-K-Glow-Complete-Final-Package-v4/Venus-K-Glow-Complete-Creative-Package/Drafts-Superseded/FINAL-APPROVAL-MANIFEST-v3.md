# Venus K-Glow — Final Approval Manifest

## Approved direction

The approved system combines the Venus Aesthetics black, cream, taupe and champagne foundation with restrained Korean-modern accents: coral, teal, violet, cobalt and holographic chrome. Use clipped corners, straight grids, bilingual typography and portrait-led imagery. Do not use waves, flowing lines, swirls, marbling, countdowns or dates.

## PR box: locked contents

The PR box has a two-stage reveal:

1. The lid contains an acrylic mirror. The lower tray is completely covered by one oversized `곧 온다 / SOMETHING IS COMING` message card.
2. Beneath that card are exactly three items: one oversized black T-shirt, one chrome K handheld prop and one A5 holographic sticker sheet.

There are zero treatment cards, zero narrow cards, zero card slots, zero samples, zero vials and no extra gift items in the box.

## Clinic and event scope

- Do not renovate, repaint or rebuild any clinic.
- Use removable suspended portrait signs, freestanding weighted racks, matching handheld portrait props and portable decor only.
- Every treatment prop carries Korean first and English directly beneath it.
- The food experience is a portable Korean snack bar in the lounge only, with bilingual food labels, allergen information and halal-capable catering controls.
- Prize wheel, gift bags and glow-match desk are removed from final scope.

## Production safeguards

- The generated images are concept direction and composition references.
- Replace concept portraits with licensed real Korean-model photography before final print or publication.
- Replace all concept-rendered logo approximations with the official supplied Venus vector logo.
- Have a native Korean speaker proof every Korean phrase and line break.
- Obtain clinic approval for all ceiling clips, cords, freestanding racks and weighted bases.
- Complete physical prototypes and a clinic fit test before releasing full production quantities.

## Image-generation direction used

The final prompt set asked for photorealistic, locally manufacturable Venus Aesthetics K-Glow concepts using black, cream, champagne, taupe, controlled Korean accent colors and holographic chrome; clean geometric layouts with no waves or dates; a two-stage PR-box reveal with one full-tray message card and exactly three lower-tray contents; seven modern suspended portrait signs and seven matching handheld props with Korean and English labels; removable application inside an unchanged neutral clinic; a practical Korean snack bar; a portable three-panel photo set; and modern Korean-model social/editorial compositions. Final production must use licensed real-model photography and the official logo master.

## Archive status

Earlier explorations and rejected concepts remain preserved in the clearly labelled `Version-1-Korean-First`, `Explorations-Not-Approved` and `Drafts-Superseded` folders. They are historical reference only and are not approved for production.
