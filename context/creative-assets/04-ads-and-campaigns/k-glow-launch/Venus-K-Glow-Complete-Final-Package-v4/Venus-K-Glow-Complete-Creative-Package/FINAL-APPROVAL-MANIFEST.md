# Venus K-Glow — Version 4 Final Approval Manifest

## Authoritative final folder

Use `Version-4-FINAL-Face-Free-Props-and-Production-Sheets/` for team review and production briefing. Version 3 and earlier work remains preserved for history but is superseded.

## Approved visual direction

The system combines Venus black, cream, taupe and champagne with restrained Korean-modern coral, teal, violet, cobalt and holographic chrome. Use straight grids, clipped corners, bilingual typography and refined line icons. Do not use waves, flowing lines, swirls, marbling, countdowns, dates or `8.24`.

## PR box: locked opening sequence

1. The lid carries one acrylic mirror.
2. The first lower-tray view is one oversized `곧 온다 / SOMETHING IS COMING` card covering the entire tray.
3. Lifting that card reveals exactly three contents: one oversized black T-shirt, one chrome K handheld prop and one A5 holographic sticker sheet.

There are zero treatment cards, zero narrow cards, zero card slots, zero samples, zero vials and no substitute gifts.

## Prop system: locked rule

- Seven suspended signs and seven matching handheld paddles use no faces, portraits, skin photography or emoji characters.
- Each treatment uses one refined line icon, Korean name first, English name second and one restrained theme color.
- Suspended signs use approved removable load-rated fixings or the weighted freestanding display rack.

## Clinic and balloon scope

- Do not renovate, repaint or rebuild the clinic. All additions are removable or freestanding.
- Reception/lounge may use one controlled cluster of 15–20 matte balloons in cream, taupe, champagne and restrained accent colors.
- Treatment rooms use only a removable icon plaque, slim vinyl panel, mirror/message detail and removable washable towel sleeve.
- No balloons, food or ceiling suspension is permitted inside treatment rooms.
- Decor must remain clear of the patient, bed head, sterile surfaces, sink splash zones, device vents, handwashing and emergency access.

## Hospitality scope

The Korean snack bar is portable and lounge-only. Use bilingual food labels, allergen information, halal-capable catering, vegetarian options and correct hot/cold holding. Prize wheel, gift bags and glow-match desk remain removed.

## Production safeguards

- Concept renders communicate direction, composition and construction intent; vendors must issue exact vector artwork, CAD/dielines and prototypes.
- Replace concept-rendered logo approximations with the official supplied Venus vector logo.
- Have a native Korean speaker proof every Korean phrase and line break.
- Final campaign/model assets require licensed real Korean-model photography or properly licensed photography.
- Complete a physical prototype, clinic fit test, hygiene review and safety approval before bulk release.

## Archive status

Version 3, earlier Korean-first concepts, rejected treatment-card systems and exploratory work remain preserved in their existing folders. They are historical reference only and are not approved for production.
