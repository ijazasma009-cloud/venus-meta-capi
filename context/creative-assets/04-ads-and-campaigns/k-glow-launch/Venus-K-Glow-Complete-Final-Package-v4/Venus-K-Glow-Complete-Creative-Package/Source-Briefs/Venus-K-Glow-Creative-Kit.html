What changed

Three things were wrong.

The box
Vials were the mistake. An unlabelled vial reads as a sample, and a sample kills mystery instantly, because everyone assumes it is serum. The box now contains zero product. It is a prop kit. Nothing inside can be mistaken for a thing you rub on your face.
The look
Cream and gold serif was the wrong register. It read expensive and forgettable. The new direction is black, chrome, holo, huge Korean type, direct flash. Something people screenshot, not something people respect.
The volume
Seven daily treatment posts was too many. One carousel does all seven on launch day. Everything else is reels, reactions and street content. Roughly three posts a week, not seven.
Dropped: "K-Beauty has landed" as the launch line. The new one is one word. 왔다. It is here. That is the whole headline.
Art direction

Seoul convenience store, not Seoul spa.

촌스럽지 않게, 세게

The reference is not a luxury dermatology clinic. It is a K-pop album insert, a Seoul subway ad, a photocard, a sticker sheet, a claw machine. Loud, glossy, a bit chaotic, extremely designed.

Kill

  • Cream and ivory backgrounds
  • Didot and every thin serif
  • Soft porcelain product still life
  • Gold foil as the only accent
  • Calm spa lighting and empty space
  • Polite captions in full sentences
  • Anything that looks like a hospital

Use

  • Near-black grounds, chrome and holographic
  • Heavy grotesque and Korean gothic, oversized
  • Direct on-camera flash, hard shadows
  • Sticker layers, cut-out edges, tape, scan lines
  • Hot pink and cyan against black
  • Captions that sound like a person, not a brand
  • Korean text bigger than the English, always

The box

No product. Only props.

뭐야 이거?

Ten boxes. Everything inside is either wearable, holdable or collectable. Nothing inside explains anything. The outside is covered in Korean and carries no English at all.

The chrome K

케이

What is deliberately not in the box: no serum, no vial, no ampoule, no sample, no leaflet, no price list, no product name, no English. If a creator can work out what we sell from the box, the box has failed.

The box itself

Outside
Matte black rigid magnetic box. Wrapped edge to edge in dense Korean type like a Seoul subway poster, printed in chrome foil. A single oversized holographic K. No logo, no English, no address label visible on the lid.
Inside lid
One line only, chrome foil on black: 8월 24일. Nothing else. They will google it.
Fill
Black shredded tissue and a holo crinkle layer, not foam. It has to make noise and move when they dig through it.
Outer
Plain black mailer, hot pink tape printed with Korean. The tape is the first frame of the video, so it has to look right before the box is even open.
Unboxing

What we actually ask them to do.

열어봐

One page, sent with the box. Short enough that they read it. The less we ask for, the more usable what comes back is.

Do
Film opening it. React honestly. Hold the chrome K up to camera at least once. Say one line: "뭐야 이거?" or the English, what even is this.
Do
Twist the capsule open on camera and read the number out loud. 8.24.
Do
Ask your audience what they think it is. That is the only question in the whole brief.
Do not
Do not guess the brand or the treatment on camera, even if you work it out. Do not tag Venus yet. Tag only the K.
Do not
Do not explain anything. You genuinely do not know what this is, and that is the content.
When
Post between 19 and 21 August. Anything after the 22nd is worthless, so pay for the date, not just the post.
Tell the PR agency twice: creators must not be sent a product sheet, a brand deck, or a treatment list before the 23rd. Agencies send those by reflex. If it goes out, the mystery is dead and so is the box.

Prompt library

Thirty prompts, ready to paste.

Paste straight into Higgsfield. Suggested model is nano_banana_pro for anything with text or fine detail, since it holds type best. Every prompt is written with no people in frame, because every human shot comes from the real shoot on the 23rd and never from AI.

Korean text in generated images will come out wrong. Image models mangle hangul, reliably. Treat every Korean word in these renders as a placeholder for position and weight only, then have your designer set the real type over the top. Never send a generated image to print with its AI-made Korean still on it.
