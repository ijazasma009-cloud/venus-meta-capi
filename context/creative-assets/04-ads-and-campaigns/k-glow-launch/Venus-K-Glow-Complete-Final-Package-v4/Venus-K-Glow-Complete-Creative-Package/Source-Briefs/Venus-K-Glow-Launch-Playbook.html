Phase 1Ships Mon 17 Aug · lands Wed 19 Aug

The box that starts everything.

무엇일까요?

Ten boxes, one per creator. The box has one job: make their audience ask "what is it?" before we have said a single word about what we sell.

The mechanicWhy it creates the question

The trick
The box does not tell them what it is. Seven unlabelled vials and a sealed envelope stamped 열지 마세요 · 24.08, meaning do not open until the 24th. They film the unboxing, hit a sealed envelope they are not allowed to open, and their audience has to wait with them.
What is inside
Seven frosted vials, Korean numerals on the caps, no labels. The sealed envelope. A small mirror etched 유리피부 so they film their own skin in it. A gold card with only a serif K.
What they do
One unboxing video, one story set. The brief gives them one question to ask their audience: "이게 뭘까요?", meaning what do you think this is. Nothing else. No product claims, because there is no product information to claim.
The envelope
Opened on camera on Monday 24 August, launch morning. Inside is the reveal card naming all seven and their invite to the shoot. That gives you a second wave of content on launch day for free.
The rule
No creator is told what the treatments are before the 23rd. Their confusion on camera is the asset. Brief the PR agency on this explicitly, because agencies default to sending a full product sheet.
Brief for the PR agency. Ten creators, Lahore-weighted since the shoot is at the clinic. Mixed tiers: two large, five mid, three small but high-engagement. Skincare and lifestyle, not fashion. At least three who already post about Korean skincare. Everyone must be free on Sunday 23 August, that is the non-negotiable filter, ask it first.

Phase 3Sunday 23 August · shoot day

The shoot.

촬영일

Ten creators, one clinic, all seven treatments filmed. This is not an event and there is no audience. It is a production day that happens to be extremely postable.

Before they arrive
Korean set fully built and lit. Hanging signs up. Props laid out. Seven vials staged on the glass shelf. Nothing gets assembled while a creator is in the building.
On arrival
Before photos, every creator, no exceptions. Same light, same three angles, no makeup, date in frame. This is the entire proof engine for the next six weeks and it cannot be recreated later.
The envelope
Still sealed. They arrive still not knowing. Film them being told what the seven are, on camera, for the first time.
Treatments
Spread across the ten so all seven are covered by at least one face, and the two most visual (K-Glass, K-Lumi Eyes) get two each.
Props in every video
Handheld paddles used in every creator's piece. It is what makes ten different videos look like one campaign.
Also shot that day
Doctor explainers for all seven. The wall of seven. Room B-roll. Enough vertical cutdowns to cover the first two weeks.
Embargo
Nothing from the shoot posts until Monday morning. Stories from the day are allowed and encouraged, but no treatment reveals and no product names.
Sunday stories are part of the hype, not a leak. Behind the scenes, the set, the props, creators arriving, gold envelopes still sealed. It keeps Sunday loud without spending the reveal.

Props

What hangs, and what they hold.

Two prop sets. One hangs in every clinic and travels the tour. One goes in creators' hands so ten different videos look like one campaign.

Order both sets by Thursday 13 August. Acrylic cutting and printing is the second-longest lead time after the boxes, and the props need to be at the clinic before the set build on Saturday the 22nd.
The set

How the clinic should look.

서울 감성

One corner of each clinic becomes a Seoul clinic. It is the backdrop for the shoot, for every Sunday party, and for every piece of content for six weeks, so it is worth building properly once.

Modular
Build it as panels that break down and travel, not as a fixed fit-out. It has to be in Karachi seven days after Lake City.
Lighting
Warm cove light and one soft key. Turn the clinic's white overheads off in the corner. Nothing kills a Korean set faster than clinical ceiling light.
Permanent bits
The seven hanging signs stay up in every clinic after the tour. They are what keeps the range visible once the campaign ends.
Content engine

What we make, on repeat.

Six formats that keep producing after launch week. Rotate them so the feed never goes flat between Sundays.
