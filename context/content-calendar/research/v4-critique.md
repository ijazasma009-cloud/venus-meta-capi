## 1. MISSING, in order of how much it changes the calendar

**There is no calendar.** Seven repeating slots times 13 weeks is a rota. What is absent: 92 dated rows, a hook per row, the specific shot needed per row, the asset that fills it, the branch that shoots it, the owner, and a status. The asset inventory in the whole document is one line, "30 doctor FAQ videos." The claim that five of seven weekly slots are near-zero marginal cost rests on footage nobody has audited across 8 branches (inference). Until that audit exists, the entire justification for daily posting is unevidenced.

**The platform's actual Pakistani composition is not in the document, and it breaks the diagnosis.** Instagram's ad audience in Pakistan is 22.4m, 34.9% female and 63.0% male; Facebook is 52.9m at 79.2% male; TikTok is 79.9m adults at 71.2% male (late 2025, https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-pakistan). Three consequences the playbook never draws. First, roughly two in three people a cold-reach reel is served here are structurally not the buyer, which explains 2,000,000 views and 22 likes better than any of the four reasons given. Second, Facebook carries 2.4x Instagram's Pakistani reach and appears nowhere in the plan. Third, the majority audience is male and the clinic sells two treatments with real male demand, body and beard-area laser reduction and PRP hair loss. Pakistani competitors already run dedicated male pages for this (https://www.cosmetique.com.pk/laser-hair-removal-for-men.php, https://3dlifestyle.pk/laser-hair-removal-for-men/, https://www.glamorousclinic.com.pk/laser-hair-removal-for-men-in-islamabad/, https://caviar.pk/laser-hair-removal/laser-hair-removal-for-men/). There is not one male-directed post in 91, and every written example is female-framed.

**Ramadan is missing, and it sits inside the plan's central arithmetic.** Track B sells a session map for March to June 2027 functions. Ramadan 2027 is expected to begin around 8 February 2027 with Eid al-Fitr around 10 March 2027, subject to the Ruet-e-Hilal Committee (https://islamiccalc.com/ramadan-2027, https://en.dailyqudrat.pk/97878/). Every 4 to 6 week laser course started in October to December runs its final sessions into or across Ramadan, and clinic hours shift then (inference). The one calculation the whole quarter rests on has an unacknowledged month-long hole in it.

**Smog is treated as a content theme and not as the thing that will close the branches.** Punjab has previously imposed a health emergency, moved schools online across divisions, banned construction in Lahore, restricted heavy vehicles, mandated masks in public, and ordered markets shut by 8pm with healthcare facilities explicitly exempt (https://www.thenews.com.pk/latest/1251321-punjab-imposes-health-emergency-as-multan-lahore-choke-on-smog, https://tribune.com.pk/story/2509978/lahore-extends-smog-restrictions-schools-closed-until-november-24). The stated branch hours are 11am to 8pm. November's highest-utility post is "healthcare is exempt, we are open, here is today's schedule," and it is not in the plan. Nor is any shoot-day contingency.

**Islamabad gets a caption footer for 13 weeks.** The November spine is Lahore-only, while Pak-EPA has called Islamabad's air "very unhealthy" with brick kilns and Rawalpindi spillover named as sources (https://www.dawn.com/news/1872628), and the playbook's own source puts Islamabad wedding spend at Rs 753,000 against Karachi's Rs 329,000. Either the weekly card carries two readings or Islamabad needs its own recurring pillar.

**Measurement stops at the platform boundary.** The KPI list ends at "DM starts and WhatsApp taps." No UTM scheme, no per-branch or per-post trackable WhatsApp deep link, no cost per consult, no consult-to-treatment rate, no show rate, no revenue attributed to a pillar. Pakistan converts on WhatsApp and the local API ecosystem is mature (https://wetarseel.ai/whatsapp-business-api-in-pakistan/, vendor source). The client already runs a CRM and a content board with three named staff, so the missing artefact is the join between post ID and lead source, not more Instagram metrics.

**Nothing for existing patients.** Clinic revenue is course completion and rebooking. There are 91 posts and zero aftercare-between-sessions, zero adherence, zero reactivation, no Broadcast Channel or patient-Stories plan. That is the cheapest revenue in the quarter (inference).

**No ruling on spoken language.** Roman Urdu is banned in text and every example is English. The 30 FAQ videos almost certainly have Urdu audio. Does Urdu audio with English burned-in subtitles satisfy the ban? Without an answer, 13 Friday posts are unproducible.

**No approval gate.** Refusal content, before-and-after content and price content are handed to a three-person team with consent principles but no named approver per post, no consent register keyed to asset ID, and no takedown SLA.

## 2. Generic, and the sharper version

- "Report saves, sends, profile visits instead of views" is what every agency says. Sharper: publish the absolute pass marks now. At current reach and a 0.06% Health and Beauty per-post benchmark, state the save count, send count and profile-visit count each pillar must hit, and the number at which a pillar gets cut. Without thresholds, "judge nothing for 4 to 6 weeks" becomes never judging anything.
- "Open on motion, no branding in 3 seconds" is table stakes. The actual differentiator here is parameter visibility. Wavelength, pass count, device screen, gauge, batch label. A backstreet clinic cannot show those. Make that the visual signature of every pillar, not just The Tray.
- "Withhold one thing per post" is stated as a principle and never operationalised. Sharper: three named withholds only, patch-test range, candidacy, machine settings, rotated on a fixed schedule so the same withhold does not read as evasion twice in one week.
- "Build every post to be forwarded" is asserted, then only Sunday is actually built that way. Either name the recipient archetype per pillar and write the send line into each template, or drop the claim.
- Nine pillars for three people across 8 branches is nine production kits. Rank them. Pillars 7 and 8 die on 1 January. Say which three survive into Q1 2027 so the team invests in reusable setups.
- The competitor work is generic in the wrong direction: ten of the twelve accounts studied are Indian or US. The direct rivals are Pakistani and unexamined: Cosmetique, 3D Lifestyle, Enfield Royal, Glamorous Clinic, Glam Aesthetics Islamabad, Caviar, SLA, Bioflex, Boston Aesthetics Lahore (URLs above plus https://www.enfieldroyalclinics.com.pk/best-laser-hair-removal-for-men-in-islamabad/, https://bostonaestheticslahore.com/2025/03/25/laser-hair-removal-in-lahore/). Teardown of their cadence, price display and male content beats another look at LaserAway.
- The English-only keyword block collides with how people search. Banning Roman Urdu also removes the transliterated terms real users type. Sharper: keep captions English, and run the search block as a tested variable rather than a rule.

## 3. Likely wrong or risky for Pakistan specifically

- **Organic content is not governed by Meta's Ad Standards.** Pillars 4 and 8 and half the hook rules are built on the Health and Wellness *ad* standard. Organic reels fall under Community Standards and sensitive-content controls (inference). Using the ad rulebook as the organic rulebook is over-restrictive in places and, worse, gives false comfort that a "Meta permitted" before-and-after is organically safe.
- **Exosomes.** They are in the treatment list and in the hashtag set with zero analysis. No exosome product is FDA-approved for hair loss as of 2026, marketing exosome therapy for regrowth is described as crossing into drug or biologic claims, and there are safety communications and reported infections from non-validated preparations (https://djholtlaw.com/before-you-offer-exosome-therapy-understand-the-laws/, law firm; https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/der2.70071, 2026 review). Pakistani status unverified. Ten numbered points go to Botox naming and the riskiest item in the list gets a hashtag.
- **"Publish a from-price" is the weakest-supported recommendation in the document** and rests on one competitor page. It puts a comparable number in frame against undercutting backstreet clinics, it cannot be honest across 8 branches at two city price levels unless one price list is confirmed, and a per-session price next to a published mean of 8.9 sessions tells the prospect her course costs about nine times that figure. If pricing goes out, publish a course band, on one branch, as a test.
- **The AQI sourcing is mixed and one leg is contested.** Punjab's own AQI scale has been criticised as too lax, reading "Satisfactory" where the US scale reads "Unhealthy", and was challenged in court in 2019 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_pollution_in_Lahore). A weekly card built on the government dashboard will understate what the audience sees in their own apps; a card quoting IQAir puts the clinic inside a live argument about the data. Pick one source, print it on the card, hold it for 13 weeks.
- **Carousel-with-music may not be available.** Business accounts are limited to the Meta Sound Collection rather than the full licensed library, and access varies by territory and account type (https://www.facebook.com/business/help/402084904469945). Verify inside the Venus account in Pakistan before building 13 carousels on the Reels-tab route.
- **Zero single images for 13 weeks is over-applied.** Holiday hours and branch address are exactly the jobs a static image does best. The rule belongs on the reach pillars, not the account.
- **PHC registration is a prerequisite, not a detail.** Registration with the Punjab Healthcare Commission is mandatory for healthcare service providers with a fine up to PKR 500,000 (http://punjablaws.gov.pk/laws/2434.html). Pillar 6 broadcasts legality. If any of the 8 branches is unregistered or mid-renewal, that pillar invites an inspection. Separately, the "Punjab Medical Council 2020 advertising guidelines" that surface in search are Indian Punjab, not Pakistani, so do not import them.
- **The December review-count act can backfire.** Audit all 8 GMB listings first. A branch sitting on 14 reviews and 4.1 gets published alongside the strong ones.
- **Smog tone needs a second rule.** Never run the smog card on a day the government declares an emergency or closes schools; switch to the utility post. Weekly damage messaging during a declared health emergency reads as trading on a crisis (judgement).

## 4. Not researched at all

1. **Pakistan's own rules on cosmetic advertising by clinics and practitioners.** The document has AHPRA, CAP, ASA, Meta and an Indian compliance blog, and for Pakistan only crackdown coverage plus DRAP. The PMDC Code of Ethics is published but its advertising and before-and-after provisions were never retrieved (https://pmdc.pk/Publication/LawsAndRegulations). PMDC is currently pursuing disciplinary action against doctors and took formal notice of filming during a procedure at a Lahore hospital (https://www.dawn.com/news/1986841, https://www.24newshd.tv/17-Sep-2026/pma-condemns-pmdc-decision-take-disciplinary-action-15-doctors). That second item is directly about filming inside a clinical setting, which is what six of the nine pillars require. This is the gap that can end the account.
2. **Facebook and TikTok**, at 52.9m and 79.9m Pakistani reach against Instagram's 22.4m. No cross-posting policy, no decision, no mention.
3. **Publish times.** 92 posts with no time of day and no day-part logic for Pakistan.
4. **Paid.** The diagnosis opens by sending the client to Ads Manager and never returns. With category organic reach structurally capped by Teen Account controls and a 0.06% benchmark, click-to-WhatsApp is probably the real growth lever and it is uncosted (inference).
5. **Production capacity.** No shoot days, no per-branch quota, no weekly output ceiling for the three named staff. 92 is asserted as cheap without one production number.
6. **Adverse events and crisis.** Review replies are covered. A burn, an infection, a viral negative reel, a PHC or SHCC inspection, and a staff-filmed clip leaking are not.
7. **South Asian data on hair shedding seasonality.** The October hair angle rests on 1996 and 1999 European cohorts, which the playbook itself flags as stale, and no regional substitute was sought.
8. **Overseas Pakistani and Gulf demand.** Part of the "wasted" international reach may be real diaspora intent. Unexamined either way.
