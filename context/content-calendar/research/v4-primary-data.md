## 1. The reach is bought, and the new data proves it

207 reels sampled, 1 Aug 2025 to 23 Sep 2026 (the reels tab serves roughly 13 months, then stops).

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| Median plays | **511,299** |
| Median likes | **67** |
| Median engagement rate | **0.058%** |
| Reels over 1,000,000 plays | 81 of 207 |
| Reels under 30,000 plays | 72 of 207 |

Now the part that matters. Filter to the last eight weeks, after the boosting was scaled back:

| Measure | Aug to Sep 2026 |
|---|---|
| Median plays | **17,546** |
| Median likes | **41** |
| Implied engagement rate | **~0.23%** |

**Organic reach for this account is roughly 5,000 to 25,000 plays.** Everything above that is paid.
When the money stopped, reach fell 29x and engagement rate improved about 4x. The million-play
posts are not reaching fans, they are reaching purchased impressions that do not respond.

Design implication: build every post to earn a response at 15,000 plays. Stop treating
2,000,000 plays with 22 likes as a success.

## 6. What the two reference reels actually do

Both are by @aashrayy0 and both run the identical formula. Verified from the API and by watching them.

| | Reel 1 [DctTDGeS6fQ](https://instagram.com/p/DctTDGeS6fQ) | Reel 2 [DdNliMKI8tx](https://instagram.com/p/DdNliMKI8tx) |
|---|---|---|
| Plays | 233,952 | 1,536,752 |
| Likes | 3,554 | 27,490 |
| **Comments** | **10,022** | **53,954** |
| Length | 34.1s | 31.3s |

**Comments exceed likes by roughly 2x.** That is the inverse of a normal post and it is entirely
deliberate. The structure, frame by frame:

1. **Open on failure.** Real screenshots of bad numbers, 652 and 1,847 views, under a red banner.
2. **Flip to success.** Real screenshots of 2.9M, 2.4M, 190K. The contrast lands inside 10 seconds.
3. **Show the method as a real document.** Raw screenshots of a checklist, not designed slides.
4. **Name the sections in big type.** A single yellow "TEXT HOOK" card as a divider.
5. **Close on a one-word comment CTA**, on screen and as the first line of the caption.
6. One static camera, face large, lav mic visible, no cuts. The overlays do all the work.

The caption architecture, both posts:
```
Line 1  the CTA, comment the magic word          <- first, not last
Line 2  the diagnosis, "your X is bad because Y"
Line 3  the consequence, "that is why you get 200 views"
Line 4  the promise of what the video delivers
        a bracketed keyword block for search
        three hashtags, no more
```

Reel 2 also shows its own CTA rule set on screen, which is worth stealing verbatim:
give ONE clear next action, matched to the goal of the post. If the goal is comments, ask an easy
but meaningful question. If saves, make it worth revisiting. If shares, name who should receive it.
If leads, create a natural next step without sounding desperate.

**Translation to a clinic.** The creator's proof is view counts because his niche is growth. The
clinic equivalent of "open on failure" is the problem the patient is living in: shaving every
second day, hair in the brush, the double chin in every photo. The equivalent of "the method as a
real document" is the actual session plan, the patch test form, the machine settings. The
equivalent of the one-word comment CTA is a genuine value exchange, not a gimmick.

This single mechanic is the direct fix for 2,000,000 plays and 22 likes: it converts a passive
view into a DM, and for a clinic a DM is the start of a booking.
