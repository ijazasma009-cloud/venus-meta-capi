# What the world's big treatment-selling clinic chains actually do
Crawled 26 Aug 2026 from a logged-in Instagram session. 7 chains, 36 recent posts each.
These are clinic chains selling treatments, not dermatology influencers.

---

## The single finding

**Patient voice beats brand voice by more than 2x, measured on the same account in the same month.**

Sono Bello, the highest engaging chain in the set:

| Post | Voice | Plays | Likes | ER |

Venus posts almost entirely in brand voice. That is the gap.

## The seven plays, with links

### 1. First-person patient story
The patient narrates their own decision, fear, session and result. The clinic is the setting,
not the speaker.
- Sono Bello, all four posts above
- LaserAway: [mother teaching her daughter self-care](https://instagram.com/p/DbWQkkDP7uk) 56k plays, 2,444 likes, **4.45% ER**, the highest single result in the audit
- LaserAway: ["Stop hiding and start shining"](https://instagram.com/p/DcOrlxTuMQ6) 2.72%
- LaserAway: ["CWM to my appointment"](https://instagram.com/p/DboqG0evGyt) 1.86%
- Milan Laser: ["I know firsthand how frustrating unwanted hair growth can be"](https://instagram.com/p/Dbbn4GlRAGA) 0.87%

### 2. Serialised patient journey, split into parts
One patient, several posts, explicit part numbers, released across weeks.
- Pulse Light London: [part one, what it really feels like](https://instagram.com/p/DbIZKAIFRJK)
- Pulse Light London: [part two, what made her finally book](https://instagram.com/p/DbYJ9SjCpZe)

**Venus already has the footage for this and has never used it.** Mehwish across 6 laser visits,
Neelum across 3 Venus Viva sessions, Ayesha Kakani across 2, Daniah across 2.

### 3. Named staff member answers one specific question
Not the founder. A named practitioner, one question, one answer.
- Ideal Image: [Lead Injector Kathleen on jowls](https://instagram.com/p/DQ7tEOBD9a4) 1.30%, their best post
- Pulse Light: ["your beauty secrets wear scrubs and wield lasers"](https://instagram.com/p/Dbn5wU_CTlj) 1.18%

### 4. "What does it REALLY feel like"
Honest sensory description of the treatment. Consistently outperforms result-only content.
- Pulse Light: [UltraClear, what it really feels like](https://instagram.com/p/DbIZKAIFRJK)
- Ideal Image: [Is microneedling worth the hype, after two sessions](https://instagram.com/p/DQxDySAjFue) 0.93%

### 5. One hero treatment, branded and repeated relentlessly
Skin Laundry has built an entire chain on one named treatment. Every post says "Signature Laser Facial".
Sono Bello does the same with "TriSculpt".
- Skin Laundry: [brunch, barbecue, sunset sail](https://instagram.com/p/Da3EFgmO1L2) 0.60%
- Skin Laundry: ["the ultimate low maintenance skincare hack"](https://instagram.com/p/DcOsYaChl8J) 0.57%
- Skin Laundry: ["15 minutes"](https://instagram.com/p/DbdqueMOmLU) 0.46%

Venus has the naming already: Venus Glow, Venus Viva, Venus Legacy, Venus Fat Freeze. It is not repeating them.

### 6. Treatment placed in life context, never in clinical context
The caption talks about brunch, errands, emails, a toddler, a wedding. The treatment is incidental.
- Skin Laundry: ["between the errands, the emails and everything else"](https://instagram.com/p/DbdqueMOmLU)
- LaserAway: [toddler mom](https://instagram.com/p/DbWQkkDP7uk)

### 7. Short, witty, treatment-led one liners
No education, no offer, just a line with personality attached to a treatment.
- Milan Laser: ["The hidden gems of laser hair removal"](https://instagram.com/p/DcZhRGkAMFZ) 0.76%
- Milan Laser: ["Laser hair removal for all skin tones? Kinda chic."](https://instagram.com/p/DcEZkQKsHT1) 0.57%
- Milan Laser: ["Nothing to worry about babe, removing hair is our specialty"](https://instagram.com/p/Db8kNP9jV5F) 1.07%
- Pulse Light: ["You're not going to believe this, but we think you should wear sunscreen. Nobody enjoys being this predictable."](https://instagram.com/p/DcBxwo1CCE2) 1.17%

### Bonus: celebrity or notable association

## What none of them do

- None of them run a discount post rail. Offers appear in bio links and stories, not as feed content.
- None of them lead with the founder. Ideal Image's best post is a staff injector, not an owner.
- None of them post polished machine footage with generic captions, which is the bulk of Venus's feed.
