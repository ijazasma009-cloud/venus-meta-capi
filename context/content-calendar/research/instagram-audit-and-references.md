# Venus Aesthetics: Instagram audit and reference library
Pulled 26 Aug 2026 from a logged-in session via Instagram's own private API.
480 Venus posts (Oct 2024 to Aug 2026) plus 12 competitor accounts.

## 1. The finding that changes the strategy

**Median engagement rate on Venus reels is 0.029 percent.**

That is not a typo. Across 160 reels with meaningful reach, the median post gets engagement from
roughly 3 people in every 10,000 who see it. Examples of the pattern:

| Post | Plays | Likes | Engagement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Face Skin Tightening, Apr 2026 | 1,988,000 | 22 | 0.00% |
| Party Peel, Mar 2026 | 1,989,000 | 25 | 0.00% |
| Sweating testimonial, Jul 2026 | 1,988,000 | 23 | 0.00% |
| Eid offer POV, Mar 2026 | 2,339,000 | 23 | 0.00% |
| Self-care glow, May 2026 | 2,341,000 | 24 | 0.00% |

Two million plays and twenty two likes is not reach. It is paid distribution into an audience
that does not care. Venus is buying enormous impressions for content nobody responds to.
