# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude—I just added the "month-end-report-generator" skill. Can you create a monthly performance report for my client?

## Example Invocations

**Example 1: Full Client Report**
Hey Claude—I just added the "month-end-report-generator" skill. Here's my January data and December comparison. Target CPA is $50, monthly goal is 300 conversions. Create a client-ready monthly report.

**Example 2: Executive Summary Only**
Hey Claude—I just added the "month-end-report-generator" skill. I need a one-page executive summary for my CMO. Just the highlights and key decisions needed.

**Example 3: Slide Deck Format**
Hey Claude—I just added the "month-end-report-generator" skill. I have a monthly review meeting tomorrow. Can you format this as a slide deck outline?

**Example 4: Tough Month Report**
Hey Claude—I just added the "month-end-report-generator" skill. We missed our targets this month. Help me write an honest but professional report that shows we have a recovery plan.

**Example 5: YoY Comparison**
Hey Claude—I just added the "month-end-report-generator" skill. Here's this January vs last January. I want to show year-over-year progress to justify budget increase.

## What to Provide

**Required:**
- This month's metrics: spend, conversions, CPA/ROAS, clicks
- Comparison data: last month OR same month last year OR goals
- Month and year

**Recommended:**
- Goals/targets (budget, conversions, CPA)
- Campaign-level breakdown
- Audience type (executive, marketing director, client)
- Actions taken during the month

**Optional:**
- Top keywords/ads
- Device/geo breakdowns
- Search term insights
- External factors (seasonality, promos)

## What You'll Get

1. **Executive Summary** - 3-4 sentence overview
2. **Scorecard** - Visual metrics with traffic lights
3. **Goal Performance** - Clear hit/miss status
4. **Campaign Analysis** - Top performers and concerns
5. **Key Insights** - What we learned with implications
6. **Actions Taken** - What was done and results
7. **Next Month Plan** - Priorities and recommendations

## Output Format Options

- **Full Report** (default) - Complete PDF-ready document
- **Slide Deck** - Outline with speaker notes
- **Email** - Condensed 3-4 paragraph version
- **Executive One-Pager** - Single page summary

Specify which format when you ask!

## Pro Tips

- Export data on the 1st of the month for clean comparisons
- Include actions you took so I can connect them to results
- Mention any external factors (promos, seasonality, market changes)
- Share previous month's report so I can maintain consistent format
