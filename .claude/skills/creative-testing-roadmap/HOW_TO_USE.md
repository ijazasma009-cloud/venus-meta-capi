# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude—I just added the "creative-testing-roadmap" skill. Can you help me build a systematic creative testing plan for my ads?

## Example Invocations

**Example 1: Full Testing Roadmap**
Hey Claude—I just added the "creative-testing-roadmap" skill. Here's my current creative portfolio and performance. Build me a 6-week testing roadmap with priorities.

**Example 2: Hook Testing Plan**
Hey Claude—I just added the "creative-testing-roadmap" skill. I have a winning concept but want to test more hooks. Give me 10 hook ideas and a testing plan.

**Example 3: New Product Launch**
Hey Claude—I just added the "creative-testing-roadmap" skill. I'm launching a new product next month. What creative tests should I run to find winners fast?

**Example 4: Concept Exploration**
Hey Claude—I just added the "creative-testing-roadmap" skill. I've only tested testimonial-style ads. What other concepts should I test and in what order?

**Example 5: Limited Budget Testing**
Hey Claude—I just added the "creative-testing-roadmap" skill. I only have $5K/month for testing. How do I maximize learning with a limited budget?

## What to Provide

**Required:**
- Product/service being advertised
- Target audience description
- Platform(s) running ads on
- Current creative count and formats

**Highly Recommended:**
- Current creative performance (CTR, CPA by creative)
- What concepts/angles have been tested
- Production capacity (how many ads can you make/week)
- Testing budget

**Optional:**
- Past winners and why they worked
- Brand guidelines or constraints
- Competitor creative examples
- Customer testimonials or research

## What You'll Get

1. **Portfolio Assessment** - Current creative health and gaps
2. **Testing Priorities** - What to test first (highest impact)
3. **Phased Roadmap** - Week-by-week testing plan
4. **Test Briefs** - Specific hypothesis and specs for each test
5. **Hook Ideas Bank** - Ready-to-use hook concepts
6. **Production Schedule** - Realistic calendar matching your capacity
7. **Decision Framework** - When to call winners and what to do next

## Pro Tips

- Test one variable at a time—multi-variable tests teach nothing
- Hooks have more impact than body content—prioritize them
- Every winner should spawn 3-4 iterations
- Plan for 50% of tests to "fail"—that's learning, not waste
- Document learnings, not just results
- Concept tests before hook tests before format tests
