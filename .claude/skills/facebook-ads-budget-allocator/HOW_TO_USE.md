# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude—I just added the "facebook-ads-budget-allocator" skill. Can you help me figure out how to distribute my Facebook Ads budget?

## Example Invocations

**Example 1: Optimize Current Allocation**
Hey Claude—I just added the "facebook-ads-budget-allocator" skill. I have 5 campaigns spending $500/day total. Some are working better than others. How should I reallocate?

**Example 2: Scaling Budget**
Hey Claude—I just added the "facebook-ads-budget-allocator" skill. I'm increasing my budget from $5k to $10k per month. How should I distribute the extra spend?

**Example 3: Cutting Budget**
Hey Claude—I just added the "facebook-ads-budget-allocator" skill. I need to cut my ad spend by 30%. Which campaigns should I reduce and by how much?

**Example 4: Efficiency Improvement**
Hey Claude—I just added the "facebook-ads-budget-allocator" skill. My blended CPA is $45 and target is $35. Budget is fixed at $300/day. How do I get more efficient?

## What to Provide

**Required:**
- Campaign names and performance (spend, conversions, CPA)
- Current budget per campaign
- Target CPA or ROAS

**Recommended:**
- Performance trends (improving, stable, declining)
- Campaign types (prospecting vs retargeting)
- Historical performance at different budget levels

**Optional:**
- Audience sizes for each campaign
- Creative freshness status
- Future budget plans

## What You'll Get

1. **Current State Assessment** - Efficiency scores and categorization for each campaign
2. **Efficiency Analysis** - Marginal CPA estimates and diminishing returns indicators
3. **Reallocation Recommendations** - Specific dollar amounts to move and where
4. **Impact Projections** - Expected conversion and CPA changes
5. **Implementation Plan** - Week-by-week action items
6. **Risk Assessment** - What could go wrong and how to mitigate

## Pro Tips

- Export campaign data from Ads Manager for accurate analysis
- Include at least 14 days of data for reliable patterns
- Note any recent changes that might affect the analysis
- Don't reallocate more than 30% of any campaign's budget at once
