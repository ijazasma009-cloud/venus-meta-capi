# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude—I just added the "facebook-ads-audience-builder" skill. Can you help me build an audience strategy for my Facebook Ads?

## Example Invocations

**Example 1: New Account Setup**
Hey Claude—I just added the "facebook-ads-audience-builder" skill. I'm launching Facebook Ads for the first time. I have an email list of 5,000 people and about 2,000 monthly website visitors. What audiences should I create?

**Example 2: Scaling Existing Account**
Hey Claude—I just added the "facebook-ads-audience-builder" skill. My current LAL audiences are fatiguing. I have 50,000 purchasers in my pixel. What new audiences should I test?

**Example 3: E-commerce Full Funnel**
Hey Claude—I just added the "facebook-ads-audience-builder" skill. I need a complete audience strategy for my Shopify store. Budget is $10k/month, AOV is $75, and I get about 500 purchases per month.

**Example 4: Lead Gen Business**
Hey Claude—I just added the "facebook-ads-audience-builder" skill. I'm a B2B SaaS company trying to generate demo requests. How should I structure my audiences?

## What to Provide

**Required:**
- What you sell (product/service)
- Your goal (sales, leads, app installs)
- Approximate monthly budget
- Available data assets (website traffic, email list, customer data)

**Recommended:**
- Current audience performance (what's working, what's not)
- Customer profile (who buys from you)
- Geographic focus
- Average order value or customer value

**Optional:**
- Competitor information
- Industry/vertical specifics
- Historical performance data

## What You'll Get

1. **Audience Inventory Assessment** - What assets you have to build from
2. **Funnel-Based Architecture** - Hot, Warm, Cold audience tiers with budget allocation
3. **Custom Audience Specifications** - Exact audiences to create with rules and windows
4. **Lookalike Strategy** - Which LALs to create, percentages, and testing priority
5. **Overlap Prevention Rules** - What to exclude from what
6. **Implementation Checklist** - Step-by-step setup guide

## Pro Tips

- Have your Ads Manager open to check existing audiences
- Know your approximate website traffic and conversion volume
- If you have a customer list, know roughly how many are on it
- Think about what makes your best customers different from average ones
