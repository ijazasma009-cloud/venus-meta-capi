# Interest Targeting Expander (Meta Ads)

When existing interest audiences saturate, you need new ones. Guessing is inefficient, and Meta's own suggestions tend to stay too close to the seed.

## Required data

Two inputs: a description of the product or service, and the current interest targets with their performance (CTR, CPA, ROAS, spend).

## Analysis instructions

Act as a Meta Ads audience researcher. Generate 20 new interest targets to test, organized in 4 categories:

1. **ADJACENT INTERESTS** (5). Closely related to the current winners, likely high intent.
2. **BEHAVIORAL SIGNALS** (5). Purchase behaviors, device usage and life events that correlate with the product.
3. **AFFINITY INTERESTS** (5). Lifestyle and media interests that indicate the customer profile.
4. **CONTRARIAN PICKS** (5). Non-obvious interests that data suggests correlate, for example yoga enthusiasts who buy SaaS tools.

For each suggested interest, provide:

- The exact interest name as it appears in Meta Ads Manager
- Estimated audience size range
- Why this interest should work, with the behavioral logic
- Suggested ad set structure: test alone, or stack with other interests

Also flag any current interests that should be retired, meaning high spend and low ROAS.

## Operating notes

- Test new interests in isolated ad sets at roughly $20 to $50 per day for 5 to 7 days before judging.
- Do not stack more than 3 interests per ad set, otherwise you cannot tell which one is working.
- Interest names change and get deprecated in Ads Manager. Flag any suggestion the user should verify exists before building the ad set.
