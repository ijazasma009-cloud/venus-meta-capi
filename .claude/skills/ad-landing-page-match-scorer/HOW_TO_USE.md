# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude—I just added the "ad-landing-page-match-scorer" skill. Can you score how well my ad matches my landing page?

## Example Invocations

**Example 1: Full Match Analysis**
Hey Claude—I just added the "ad-landing-page-match-scorer" skill. Here's my Google Search ad [paste headlines, descriptions] and landing page [URL/screenshot]. Score the match and tell me what's causing my low Quality Score.

**Example 2: RSA Analysis**
Hey Claude—I just added the "ad-landing-page-match-scorer" skill. I have an RSA with 15 headlines and 4 descriptions going to this landing page [URL]. Which combinations have weak message match?

**Example 3: Social Ad Check**
Hey Claude—I just added the "ad-landing-page-match-scorer" skill. Here's my Facebook ad [attach image + copy] and the landing page it goes to [URL]. Is the visual and message continuity good?

**Example 4: Quick Headline Comparison**
Hey Claude—I just added the "ad-landing-page-match-scorer" skill. My ad says "Get 50% Off CRM Software" but my landing page headline is "Welcome to Acme Software." Is this hurting conversions?

**Example 5: Multiple Keywords**
Hey Claude—I just added the "ad-landing-page-match-scorer" skill. I have 20 keywords going to one landing page. Here's the keyword list and the page [URL]. Score the match for my top keywords.

**Example 6: Quality Score Troubleshooting**
Hey Claude—I just added the "ad-landing-page-match-scorer" skill. My Landing Page Experience rating is "Below Average" in Google Ads. Here's my ad and landing page—what's causing this?

## What to Provide

**Required:**
- Ad copy (headlines, descriptions)
- Landing page (URL or screenshots)

**Strongly Recommended:**
- Target keywords (for search ads)
- Ad platform (Google, Meta, LinkedIn)
- Campaign objective

**Optional:**
- Quality Score data
- Landing page experience rating
- Conversion rate
- Bounce rate

## What You'll Get

1. **Match Score** - Overall score out of 100 across 5 dimensions
2. **Dimension Breakdown** - Headline, Offer, Keyword, Visual, and Audience match scores
3. **Disconnect Analysis** - Specific gaps between ad promise and LP delivery
4. **Rewrite Suggestions** - Better headlines and copy that improve match
5. **Quality Score Impact** - How issues affect your Google Ads QS (if applicable)
6. **Priority Fixes** - Ranked list of changes to make first

## Pro Tips

- Share all RSA headline variations—some combinations may match better than others
- Include sitelinks and extensions—they set expectations too
- Screenshot above-the-fold AND below-the-fold for complete analysis
- Share the target keywords—message match is relative to search intent
- If you have Quality Score data, include it to validate the analysis
- For Facebook/Instagram, the visual match is especially important
