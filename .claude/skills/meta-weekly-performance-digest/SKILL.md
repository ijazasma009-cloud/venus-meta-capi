# Weekly Meta Performance Digest

Turns last week's Meta Ads data into a summary a CMO can read in two minutes without asking follow-up questions.

## Required data

This week's and last week's Ads Manager export with: campaign name, spend, impressions, CTR, CPC, conversions, CPA, ROAS, frequency. Include ad-level data for the creative section.

## Analysis instructions

Act as a senior Meta Ads strategist writing a weekly performance report. Generate a digest with these sections:

**1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY** (3 to 4 sentences)
- Top-line ROAS, spend, conversions and CPA vs. last week
- What drove the change, naming specific campaigns or ad sets
- One thing that went well, one thing to watch

**2. CAMPAIGN PERFORMANCE TABLE**
- `Campaign | Spend | ROAS | CPA | Conv | vs. Last Week (%)`
- Sort by spend descending
- Flag any campaign with more than 20% CPA increase

**3. CREATIVE PERFORMANCE**
- Top 3 performing ads by ROAS, with why they are working
- Bottom 3 ads that should be paused or refreshed
- Creative fatigue warnings: any ad with CTR declining 3 or more consecutive days

**4. AUDIENCE INSIGHTS**
- Best performing audience segment this week
- Any audience showing saturation signals, meaning frequency above 3

**5. NEXT WEEK PRIORITIES** (3 bullets)
- Specific, actionable items based on this week's data

Tone: professional but conversational. Written for a CMO with two minutes.

## Operating notes

- Every number in the digest must come from the supplied data. Never estimate or fill gaps with plausible figures. If a metric is missing from the export, say so in the report rather than inferring it.
- Week-over-week percentages on a low base are noise. Note the absolute numbers alongside the deltas.
