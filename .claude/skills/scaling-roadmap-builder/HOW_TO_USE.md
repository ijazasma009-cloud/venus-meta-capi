# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude—I just added the "scaling-roadmap-builder" skill. Can you help me plan how to grow my Google Ads from $10K to $30K/month?

## Example Invocations

**Example 1: Full Roadmap**
Hey Claude—I just added the "scaling-roadmap-builder" skill. I'm spending $8K/month at $45 CPA (target is $60). I want to get to $25K/month. Build me a scaling plan.

**Example 2: Aggressive Timeline**
Hey Claude—I just added the "scaling-roadmap-builder" skill. I need to 3x my spend in 3 months. Current: $15K/month, 200 leads, $75 CPA. Is this realistic? What's the plan?

**Example 3: Readiness Check**
Hey Claude—I just added the "scaling-roadmap-builder" skill. Before I scale, am I even ready? Here's my current account setup and performance. What should I fix first?

**Example 4: Stuck at a Plateau**
Hey Claude—I just added the "scaling-roadmap-builder" skill. I've been stuck at $20K/month for 6 months. Every time I increase budget, CPA spikes. How do I break through?

**Example 5: New Account Scale**
Hey Claude—I just added the "scaling-roadmap-builder" skill. I just launched a new account 2 months ago, spending $3K/month with good results. How do I scale to $15K?

## What to Provide

**Required:**
- Current monthly spend
- Current conversions
- Current CPA or ROAS
- Target spend level (or target conversions)

**Recommended:**
- Campaign breakdown (which campaigns perform best)
- Target CPA/ROAS
- Timeline expectations
- Business context (leads, sales, capacity)

**Optional:**
- Historical scaling attempts
- Impression share data
- Competitive context
- Market size estimates

## What You'll Get

1. **Readiness Assessment** - Are you ready to scale?
2. **Phase-by-Phase Plan** - Specific actions for each growth stage
3. **Timeline & Milestones** - Realistic expectations
4. **Risk Mitigation** - What could go wrong and how to handle it
5. **Success Criteria** - How to know each phase worked
6. **Pause Triggers** - When to stop and reassess

## Pro Tips

- Be honest about your current efficiency—scaling broken campaigns just breaks them faster
- Conservative scaling (20-25% increases) is almost always better than aggressive
- Plan for CPA to increase 10-20% when scaling—budget for it
- Backend capacity matters—can your sales team handle 2x the leads?
