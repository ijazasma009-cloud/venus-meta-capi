# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude—I just added the "facebook-ads-scaling-roadmap" skill. Can you help me create a plan to scale my Facebook Ads?

## Example Invocations

**Example 1: Double Spend**
Hey Claude—I just added the "facebook-ads-scaling-roadmap" skill. I'm spending $3k/month and want to get to $6k/month. Current CPA is $35. Help me create a scaling plan.

**Example 2: Aggressive Growth**
Hey Claude—I just added the "facebook-ads-scaling-roadmap" skill. We have a product launch in 6 weeks and need to 3x our ad spend. What's the plan?

**Example 3: First-Time Scaling**
Hey Claude—I just added the "facebook-ads-scaling-roadmap" skill. I've never scaled past $200/day—every time I try, CPA explodes. Can you help me do it properly?

**Example 4: Q4 Preparation**
Hey Claude—I just added the "facebook-ads-scaling-roadmap" skill. Black Friday is in 8 weeks and I need to scale from $500/day to $2000/day. Build me a roadmap.

## What to Provide

**Required:**
- Current daily/monthly spend
- Current CPA or ROAS
- Target spend level
- Timeline (how fast do you need to scale?)

**Recommended:**
- Current campaign/audience structure
- Top performing campaigns and creative
- Past scaling attempts and what happened
- Creative production capacity

**Optional:**
- LTV data (to calculate acceptable CPA)
- Team capacity
- Historical performance at different spend levels

## What You'll Get

1. **Scaling Overview** - Current vs target with timeline
2. **Readiness Assessment** - Are you ready to scale?
3. **Budget Progression** - Week-by-week spend increases
4. **Audience Expansion Plan** - How to expand reach
5. **Creative Requirements** - How many new ads you need
6. **Risk Mitigation** - What could go wrong and rollback plan
7. **Phase-by-Phase Roadmap** - Detailed action items

## Pro Tips

- Build your creative pipeline BEFORE scaling spend
- Expect CPA to increase 10-30% when scaling—budget for it
- Don't scale more than 30% per week if you want to maintain efficiency
- Have clear rollback triggers defined before you start
