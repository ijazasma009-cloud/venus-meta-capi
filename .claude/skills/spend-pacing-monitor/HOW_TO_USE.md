# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude—I just added the "spend-pacing-monitor" skill. Am I on track to hit my monthly budget?

## Example Invocations

**Example 1: Quick Pacing Check**
Hey Claude—I just added the "spend-pacing-monitor" skill. My budget is $25K/month. I've spent $8K and we're on day 12. Am I on track?

**Example 2: Full Pacing Report**
Hey Claude—I just added the "spend-pacing-monitor" skill. Here's my daily spend for January so far. Budget is $30K. Give me a full pacing analysis with projections.

**Example 3: Campaign-Level Pacing**
Hey Claude—I just added the "spend-pacing-monitor" skill. Here's spend by campaign. Which campaigns are underspending their budgets?

**Example 4: End-of-Month Rescue**
Hey Claude—I just added the "spend-pacing-monitor" skill. I have $12K left to spend and only 5 days left. How do I pace this without tanking CPA?

**Example 5: Overspend Alert**
Hey Claude—I just added the "spend-pacing-monitor" skill. We're on day 18 and already at 85% of budget. What should I do?

## What to Provide

**Required:**
- Monthly budget target
- Month-to-date spend
- Current date (day of month)

**Recommended:**
- Daily spend breakdown
- Campaign-level spend and budgets
- Days remaining in month

**Optional:**
- Historical spending patterns
- Known upcoming events (promos, holidays)
- Conversion data (for efficiency context)

## What You'll Get

1. **Pacing Status** - Are you on track, under, or over?
2. **Projections** - Where will you end up at current rate?
3. **Daily Analysis** - Patterns and anomalies in spend
4. **Campaign Breakdown** - Which campaigns are off-pace?
5. **Recommendations** - What to adjust to hit target
6. **Scenarios** - What happens if you do X vs Y

## Pro Tips

- Check pacing weekly minimum—daily is better for large budgets
- Early month variance is normal—don't panic until day 10+
- Late month adjustments are harder—catch issues by day 15
- Know your day-of-week patterns—weekends often differ
