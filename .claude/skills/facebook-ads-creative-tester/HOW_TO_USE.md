# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude—I just added the "facebook-ads-creative-tester" skill. Can you help me design a proper A/B test for my Facebook Ads?

## Example Invocations

**Example 1: Video Hook Test**
Hey Claude—I just added the "facebook-ads-creative-tester" skill. I want to test whether a question hook or a bold statement hook performs better in my video ads. My current CTR is 1.2% and I spend $200/day. Help me design this test.

**Example 2: Image vs UGC Test**
Hey Claude—I just added the "facebook-ads-creative-tester" skill. I want to test professional product photos against UGC-style images. Current CPA is $45 with about 5 conversions/day.

**Example 3: Copy Length Test**
Hey Claude—I just added the "facebook-ads-creative-tester" skill. Should I test short copy vs long copy? My current ads have medium-length copy and convert at 2.1%. Budget is $150/day.

**Example 4: Full Creative Concept Test**
Hey Claude—I just added the "facebook-ads-creative-tester" skill. I have 3 completely different creative concepts I want to test. They're all different - how should I approach this?

## What to Provide

**Required:**
- What you want to test (the variable)
- Current performance baseline (CTR, CPA, conversion rate)
- Your hypothesis or what you're trying to learn

**Recommended:**
- Daily budget available for testing
- Current conversion volume
- How quickly you need results

**Optional:**
- Past test results and learnings
- Creative production capabilities
- Specific ads or concepts you want to test

## What You'll Get

1. **Hypothesis Statement** - Clear, testable hypothesis
2. **Test Variants** - Exactly what to test and how they differ
3. **Statistical Requirements** - Sample sizes and confidence levels
4. **Timeline** - How long to run the test
5. **Budget Plan** - Daily and total budget needed
6. **Campaign Setup** - Exact settings to use
7. **Success Criteria** - How to determine a winner
8. **Documentation Template** - Where to record learnings

## Pro Tips

- Always test on a proven audience (don't test creative AND audience simultaneously)
- Don't use CBO for tests—it will favor one variant too early
- Wait for statistical significance, not gut feeling
- Document learnings even from failed tests—knowing what doesn't work is valuable
